Lightning Tab Groups
Manual Shortcuts

Tab groups, one keystroke away.

A lightweight Firefox tab group manager. Switch between tab groups and search your tabs using only the keyboard. No mouse, no hunting through a crowded tab strip.

Add to Firefox or read the online help

Your hands stay on the keys.

Open the popup, pick a group, press Enter. Everything else, from moving tabs to closing a whole group, is a shortcut too.

  • 01

    Switch with the keyboard

    Jump straight to a group with 1–9 or a–z.

  • 02

    Colored mnemonics

    Give each group a color and recall it by number, the same way every time.

  • 03

    Emoji as names

    Start a group's name with an emoji and spot it at a glance, even in a long list.

  • 04

    Close or reload a whole group

    CtrlDelete closes every tab in the selected group; / reloads them. Closed one by mistake? CtrlZ brings it back.

  • 05

    Copy groups between browsers

    Copy a group to the clipboard with PageUp and paste it into another Firefox with PageDown.

  • 06

    Search tabs from the keyboard

    Press = to search your tabs and find the one you want without touching the mouse.

Everything is a key.

Every command in the popup has a key. Here is the full list.

Ctrl↑↓move the selected tab group up or down
←→hide or show the tabs of the selected tab group
Enterswitch to the selected tab group
1–9 a–zswitch directly to a tab group by its number or letter
Insertcreate a tab group
CtrlInsertedit the selected tab group
Deletedelete the selected tab group
CtrlDeleteclose all tabs in the selected tab group
CtrlZrestore the most recently deleted tab group
Homemove the current tab to the selected tab group
Endmove all visible tabs, or only the highlighted ones, to the selected tab group
ShiftEndmove the tabs of the selected tab group to a new window
CtrlEnd CtrlHomesame as Home and End, then switch to that tab group
PageUpcopy the selected tab group to the clipboard
ShiftPageUpcopy all tab groups to the clipboard
PageDownpaste tab groups from the clipboard
/reload all tabs in the selected tab group
=search tabs
